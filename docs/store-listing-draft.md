# FilterFit store listing — draft (not decided)

**Status: draft for Mike's decision.** Nothing here is final — least of all
the price. The listing is written to separate FilterFit from the apps it will
sit next to in search, and the monetisation section lays out the options with
a recommendation, not a decision.

## Why this positioning

Web research (Lab report 76) found no FilterFit coverage — only unrelated
apps sharing the name "Filter" / "Filter Game":

- **Filter Game** (com.cnstudio.filter.game) — 2.9★, a TikTok mini-game app.
- **Games Filter** (com.games.effect.tiktok) — 3.8–3.9★, TikTok filter games.
- **AR Games: Ranking Filter** — another TikTok filter-game app.

Their reviews repeat the same complaints: ads, and games that aren't there.
FilterFit is none of those things — it is a real photo tool, not a game — so
the listing says so in the first line, twice, in plain words. Anyone
searching "filter" will see game apps first; the listing has to make the
difference obvious before the first screenshot.

## Monetisation — options, and a recommendation (Mike decides)

| Model | How it works | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free, no ads** | Everything free, no ads, no paywall. | Fastest to try; nothing like the ad-heavy clones; reviews stay clean. | No revenue; a preset/filter pack has to come later to earn anything. |
| **Free with ads** | Free app, banner or interstitial ads. | Earns from day one. | Puts FilterFit in exactly the bucket the clone reviews complain about; an ad in the middle of an edit kills the "one good pick, fast" promise. |
| **Paid up front (one-off)** | One price to download. | Clean, no ads, no paywall friction later. | Hardest to get tried at all — competes with free built-in phone editors; no way to try before paying. |
| **Free + one-off preset/filter pack** | Core app free, no ads; extra preset packs or filter packs as one-off purchases. | Free to try, no ads, earns from the people who love it. | Needs the packs built; the free core has to be good enough alone. |

**Recommendation (not a decision): free, no ads, with a one-off preset/filter
pack later.** Reasons: the Lab's red flag is that FilterFit competes directly
with free built-in phone editors — charging up front makes that fight harder,
and ads put it in the same bucket as the 2.9★ clones whose reviews complain
about ads. Free + no ads is the fastest way to get tried, and the preset pack
gives the people who love it something to pay for without ever interrupting
an edit. The price of the pack is NOT set here — that is Mike's call, after
the blind test proves the recommendation is worth paying for.
